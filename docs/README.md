# Knowledge Base

Everything decided about the ALTERED rebrand: domain, wordmark, and brand mark. Written so a cold chat can resume without the original conversation. Operating rules live in the root `AGENTS.md`.

Last updated: 2026-08-16.

## Status

The mark is in refinement, not finished. The thesis, the grid, and most parameters are locked. What remains open is the inflection-point proportions, the final frame/bar weight, and the typeface licence purchase.

## Read in this order

1. `BRAND.md` - the rebrand decisions, what the mark has to mean, and the palette.
2. `MARK.md` - the icon: canonical grid, locked parameters, construction, and the full rejected list with reasons.
3. `TYPE.md` - typefaces, metrics, and the lockup mathematics.
4. `PIPELINE.md` - how the studies are generated and rendered, and how to reproduce or extend them.

## Hard constraints carried through every round

- The operator reviews every round and rejects by name. Do not re-propose a rejected direction without new reasoning.
