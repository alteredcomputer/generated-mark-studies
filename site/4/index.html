ROUND 4 / REFINEMENT

The thesis is settled. This round varies exactly one thing at a time against a fixed base: expo-out taper, floating inside a frame, throat and inflection centred at 50%. Twelve inflection-point stars, ten easings including the 45-degree mirrors, and sweeps for frame weight, attachment, bar weight, inflection position and star reach. Wordmark is real Berkeley Mono.

S / Inflection point

Twelve stars against one base. Straight Kortex spikes, tangent flares that leave the bar at zero degrees, and the asterisk rotated so a stroke fuses with the output.

E / Easing

Six Out curves with their formulas, then four In curves. Reflecting an Out easing about the 45 degree line yields its In twin, so these four are the mirrors you asked about.

F / Frame weight

Border thickness as a fraction of the field, from 6.3% to 16.7%.

A / Attachment

Whether the flow floats inside the container, meets only the left wall, or fills it edge to edge with no margin.

B / Bar weight

Output thickness swept against a fixed frame.

P / Inflection position

Where the throat and star sit across the window.

R / Star reach

Spike length as a fraction of the window half height.

O / Oscilloscope

The instrument reading: a narrow solid strip with two or three small square voids instead of knobs, and the screen vertically centred.

X / Small-size cut

The 16px derivative: container removed, negative space filled, flow punched out.
